Project video, 2017

A reconnaissance robot explores and maps a maze it has never seen

A robot is put into a maze nobody has surveyed, with a laser range finder, two cameras and no map. It decides for itself where to go next, and comes out with the floor plan it drew on the way.

Laser range finder + stereo visionUniversity of Peradeniya, 2017

The 2017 Peradeniya project video. A holonomic robot with a laser range finder and stereo cameras explores a plywood maze it has never seen, builds the occupancy map in the inset, and finishes with the stitched 3D reconstruction of the arena. The localisation and control segment, which was mine, starts at 0:59.

What you are seeing

The sensing half is stereo, not depth hardware

From 0:32 the video follows the vision pipeline: camera calibration against a checkerboard, disparity maps computed from the stereo pair, depth extracted from those, and the per-view point clouds stitched into one cloud of the arena. A laser range finder supplies the metric ranges the vision is fused with. Sachini Ekanayake built this part.

The planning half decides where to go next

From 0:44 the exploration planning system is shown on the occupancy grid it plans over: the unknown environment as the robot sees it, the smallest gap first rule, adaptive stepping, and the neighbour search that produces the next candidate. Nilanga Thelasingha built this part.

Localisation and control, the part I built

From 0:59 the segment is mine. A particle filter localises the robot in the map it is building, and the video shows the particle cloud collapsing onto a single hypothesis and the recovered trajectory over the arena loop. I was the SLAM and control developer on the project, one of three students.

Then it is put in the maze

The plywood arena is built on camera, and from 1:24 the run is filmed from above with the occupancy map inset in the corner. The map fills in corridor by corridor as the robot commits to one frontier at a time, and the video closes on the stitched 3D reconstruction of the whole arena.
