Projects · Part I

M2H

Four dense tasks in one model, replacing two separate networks that did not agree with each other.

2025 · published

What it does

Window-based cross-task attention on a DINOv2-small backbone, predicting depth, semantics, surface normals, and edges together so that each task can use what the others found.

Key numbers

  • 61.54 mIoU and 0.4196 RMSE on NYUDv2
  • 0.11 m ITC mean error at 30 fps
  • 81 M parameters, 488 GFLOPs

Paper

M2H: Multi-Task Learning with Efficient Window-Based Cross-Task Attention for Monocular Spatial Perception
