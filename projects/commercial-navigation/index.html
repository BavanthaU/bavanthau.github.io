01 — The system

What it does

Service robots localise, map their venues and support visitor guidance in public environments. The software integrates ROS, GMapping, LiDAR and 3D obstacle sensing with vendor SDKs and voice assistance.

Engineering ownership

My contribution

  • Built navigation software and integrated sensing, mapping and vendor interfaces.
  • Led a three-engineer team and deployments of 30+ robots for 10+ enterprise clients, including a six-month run at Expo 2020 Dubai.
  • Recovered localisation on site as crowd conditions changed and supported customer operations.
