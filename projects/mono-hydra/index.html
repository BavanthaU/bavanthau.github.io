Projects · Part I

Mono-Hydra

Where it started. The first real-time 3D scene graph built from a monocular camera and an IMU.

2022 to 2023 · published

What it does

Learned depth and learned semantics run as separate networks, feeding a robocentric square-root VIO and the Hydra scene graph layers: mesh, objects, places, rooms, buildings.

Key numbers

  • 0.19 m and 0.21 m ITC mean error
  • 15 fps at 848x480
  • RTX 3080 8GB laptop
