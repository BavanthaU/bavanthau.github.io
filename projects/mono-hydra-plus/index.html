Projects · Part I

Mono-Hydra++

The current system. One camera, one IMU, a hierarchical 3D scene graph, and a perception loop that feeds back into state estimation.

2025 to 2026 · under review

What it does

Predicted sparse depth enters the visual-inertial odometry as metric anchor factors. Semantic masks keep dynamic and unreliable regions out of the estimator. The poses that come back out align the next dense predictions in time. Perception feeds the estimator and the estimator feeds perception.

Key numbers

  • 0.08 m ITC 2nd floor mean error
  • 0.033 m calibrated ATE on 7-Scenes
  • 44.96 % semantic mesh mIoU on ScanNet
