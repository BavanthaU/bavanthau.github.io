01 — The system

What it does

Predicted sparse depth enters the visual-inertial odometry as metric anchor factors. Semantic masks keep dynamic and unreliable regions out of the estimator. The poses that come back out align the next dense predictions in time. Perception feeds the estimator and the estimator feeds perception.

01 / Inside Mono-Hydra++

Align predictions before fusing the map.

A pixel in the previous frame does not refer to the same scene point after the camera moves. RVIO2 relative poses warp historical depth and labels into the current view. The nearest sample is retained at each pixel; accepted history is fused with the current prediction using depth medians and label voting.

The output is aligned depth and labels for local graph refinement and volumetric fusion.

02 / Inside Mono-Hydra++

Turn selected depth samples into pose constraints.

Aligned depth is sampled at tracked keypoints. Depth validity, motion, reprojection and semantic checks select the samples that can become depth factors. These enter local graph refinement alongside visual tracks, RVIO2 motion and IMU constraints.

Refined poses and loop-closure proposals are passed to Hydra, which optimises the map and updates the scene graph.

03 / Inside Mono-Hydra++

Constrain optical-axis depth in the local graph.

The depth measurement is camera-frame distance along the optical axis. A depth factor connects a camera pose and a tracked landmark; visual, motion and IMU factors provide complementary constraints.

The illustration omits velocity and bias variables to make the pose–landmark relationship easier to read.
