Spatial understanding

Mono-Hydra++

2026 manuscript · Under review

I built a monocular scene-graph mapping system that combines learned perception with visual-inertial estimation. Sparse predicted-depth factors anchor the pose estimate, while predictions are aligned across frames before fusion. The ROS 2 stack runs on the drone’s Jetson Orin NX with a ZED X One camera.

  • C++
  • ROS 2
  • Factor graphs
  • Jetson Orin NX

Multi-task perception

M2H / M2H-MX

IROS 2025 · ICRA 2026 workshop

I designed how depth, semantics, surface normals and edges share information in one network. M2H uses DINOv2 with windowed cross-task attention; M2H-MX adds LoRA-adapted DINOv3 features and register-gated Mamba context. They provide the perception front end for Mono-Hydra++.

  • PyTorch
  • DINOv2 / DINOv3
  • Multi-task learning
  • TensorRT

Belief-graph based indoor exploration

ATLAS

2025–present · Tested on the drone

I developed ATLAS to choose exploration goals using a compact belief graph of places and open ends. It learns first from a classical expert, then through reinforcement learning. Built in PyTorch and tested in Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab, it is integrated over ROS 2 with Mono-Hydra++ maps and has been tested on my custom drone.

  • Reinforcement learning
  • Belief graphs
  • Isaac Sim / Isaac Lab
  • ROS 2
