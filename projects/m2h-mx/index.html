M2H-MX

The perception front end that made the map better, and the ablation that says why.

2026 · published

What it does

DINOv3 features adapted with LoRA, decoded through register-gated Mamba blocks at multiple scales, with directed cross-task refinement between depth and semantics. Exported to ONNX and TensorRT FP16 for the drone.

Key numbers

  • 65.60 mIoU and 0.3800 RMSE on NYUDv2
  • 6.91 cm average ScanNet ATE, down from 17.59 cm
  • 25.53 fps on Jetson Orin NX 16GB at 256x192
