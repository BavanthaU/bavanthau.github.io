Autonomous exploration / ATLAS

Teach a robot where to go next.

I developed ATLAS to select exploration goals from a persistent belief graph of places, open ends and unfinished work. The learned policy ranks routes admitted by a geometric planner.

Simulation validated · Drone transfer underway

Fully trained and tested in simulation, with earlier iterations tested on the drone. The current policy is now being transferred to the aircraft.
