What the camera gives the map

M2H-MX takes one RGB frame and predicts metric depth and semantic labels together. Those two outputs are the entire input to the mapping backend. Indoors is what the system is built for; the street scene is the same model on data it was not designed for.

Selected work

  • ISPRS J. Photogramm. Remote Sens.

    Mono-Hydra++: Real-Time Monocular Scene Graph Construction with Multi-Task Learning for 3D Indoor Mapping

    A single camera and an IMU build a hierarchical metric-semantic 3D scene graph of an indoor building in real time, and the map is accurate to 0.08 m.

    Under review at ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing

  • ICRA 2026 SRRA Workshop

    M2H-MX: Multi-Task Semantic and Geometric Perception for Real-Time Monocular 3D Scene Graph Construction

    Better per-frame depth and semantics measurably improve the map and the trajectory, not just the benchmark score.

    Accepted, ICRA 2026 SRRA Workshop

  • IROS 2025

    M2H: Multi-Task Learning with Efficient Window-Based Cross-Task Attention for Monocular Spatial Perception

    Four dense prediction tasks share one small model and make each other better, fast enough to sit inside a mapping loop.

    Published, IEEE/RSJ IROS 2025

  • ISPRS Annals 2023

    Mono-Hydra: Real-Time 3D Scene Graph Construction from Monocular Camera Input with IMU

    A monocular camera and an IMU can build a layered 3D scene graph while the robot is still moving, which the field had only done with depth sensors or LiDAR.

    Published, ISPRS Annals 2023
