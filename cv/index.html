Experience

August 2022 to July 2026

Doctoral researcher, robotics and computer vision

Faculty ITC, University of Twente Enschede, Netherlands

PhD project: autonomous real-time UAV mapping with spatial perception for indoor applications. Supervised by George Vosselman and Francesco Nex.

  • Developed C++ software for monocular visual-inertial SLAM and 3D scene-graph mapping in ROS 2 (Mono-Hydra++). Sparse predicted-depth factors in the factor graph cut trajectory error by 61% on ScanNet against monocular GO-SLAM while holding 25 to 30 Hz.
  • Designed the multi-task deep learning architecture for joint depth estimation and semantic segmentation (M2H, M2H-MX). M2H appeared at IEEE/RSJ IROS 2025; M2H-MX, accepted at the IEEE ICRA 2026 SRRA Workshop, improves NYUDv2 mIoU by 6.6% and depth RMSE by 9.4% over the best tested baseline.
  • Built a custom autonomous drone and deployed onboard perception on its Jetson Orin NX 16GB, exported through ONNX and optimised with TensorRT FP16, processing images at 25.5 frames per second.
  • Developed ATLAS, an autonomous exploration algorithm on top of that mapping system: a compact belief graph of places and open ends keeps goal selection real time, trained in Isaac Sim from planner demonstrations and deep reinforcement learning, integrated with Mono-Hydra++ over ROS 2 and deployed on the drone.
  • Prepared and taught practical exercises for two master's courses in robot perception and autonomy, and supervised three master's thesis students.

The loop this PhD closes, in four clips.

  1. 01

    Perception and scene graph

    M2H-MX predicts metric depth and semantics in a single pass, and Mono-Hydra++ turns them into a hierarchical 3D scene graph. The same stack, run across different environments.

  2. 02

    Robustness on device

    The VIO running on a Jetson Orin with a ZED X One, holding pose down the stairs and back up. This is the robustness test for the algorithm.

  3. 03

    The drone

    The quadrotor built to deploy this stack, carrying and computing all of it onboard.

  4. 04

    Exploration

    Still at simulation level, in NVIDIA Isaac Sim and the lab: the map it built decides where to look next. Deployment on the drone comes next.
