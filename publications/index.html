  • Mono-Hydra++: Real-Time Monocular Scene Graph Construction with Multi-Task Learning for 3D Indoor Mapping

    U.V.B.L. Udugama, George Vosselman, Francesco Nex

    ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing Under review

    IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA) 2026, SRRA Workshop, Vienna

  • M2H: Multi-Task Learning with Efficient Window-Based Cross-Task Attention for Monocular Spatial Perception

    U.V.B.L. Udugama, George Vosselman, Francesco Nex

    IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS) 2025

    Four dense prediction tasks share one small model and make each other better, fast enough to sit inside a mapping loop.

  • Mono-Hydra: Real-Time 3D Scene Graph Construction from Monocular Camera Input with IMU

    U.V.B.L. Udugama, G. Vosselman, F. Nex

    ISPRS Annals of the Photogrammetry, Remote Sensing and Spatial Information Sciences, volume 1, pages 439 to 445

    A monocular camera and an IMU can build a layered 3D scene graph while the robot is still moving, which the field had only done with depth sensors or LiDAR.
